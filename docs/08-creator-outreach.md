# Creator outreach

## Who to DM

- 5k–50k followers on TikTok or Instagram Reels.
- At least two videos over 500k views in the last 90 days. Proof they can stop the scroll.
- Talks to camera or does clean screen-recording demos. Either works; "aesthetic vlog" doesn't.
- Niches: sober-curious / "damp lifestyle", hangxiety humour, gym and weight loss, personal
  finance ("what I stopped buying"), wellness routines, and anyone who has posted that they're
  doing Sober October or Dry January.

Aim for 30 DMs to land 10 creators. Small creators answer DMs; managers don't.

## First DM (keep it under 60 words)

```
Hey [name], your "[video title]" video is exactly the person my app is for. It's called Damp:
drink less, not never. One tap a night, a streak, and the money you didn't spend.

Would you do 3 short videos over 2 weeks? Paid, plus a bonus per trial your code drives.
Can send the app + a 1-page brief today.
```

## The brief (send as one message after they say yes)

```
THE APP
Damp asks "Dry tonight?" at 9pm. Tap Dry, or Drank and how many. It counts dry nights, the
money you kept (your own numbers) and gives you a card at 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 nights. A drinking
night is a number, not a failure.

YOUR CODE: [CODE] — free month for your audience, we track trials from it.

WHAT TO MAKE (3 videos, 7–15s each)
1. Your version of the demo: notification → tap Dry → streak ticks → money ticks.
2. Your version of one of these: "I put my honest number in" (the reveal), "Day N of Sober
   October" with the card, or the "5 signs you're drinking on autopilot" slideshow.
3. Your choice, your style.

RULES
- App on screen within 2 seconds. Text hook on frame one.
- Don't explain features. Show the tap and the number.
- Never show a drink being drunk (ad platforms reject it, and it's not the brand).
- Say the name once. No "link in bio".
- Post from your account; we may boost the post as a Spark Ad (you keep the views).

REFERENCES
[3 links to videos that already worked in this category]
[Screen recordings of the app: link to shared folder]

PAY
$[150–300] per video on posting, plus $2 per trial start from your code, paid monthly.
```

## Tracking

- One App Store offer code per creator (App Store Connect → Subscriptions → Offer Codes).
- Spreadsheet: creator, video link, post date, views at 48h, trials from code at 7 days, cost.
- Trials per $100 spent is the number. Re-book anyone above the median. Drop anyone below
  after three videos.

## Follow-ups

Day 3 no reply: "Bumping this in case it got buried. Happy to just send the app so you can try it."
Day 7 no reply: move on.
