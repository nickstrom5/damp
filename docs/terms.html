What Damp does

Damp lets you log whether each night was alcohol-free or how many drinks you had, and shows streaks, estimated money not spent and estimated calories not consumed based on numbers you enter. It is a tool to support your own choices. Estimates are estimates.

Damp is not a medical device, does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and is not a substitute for professional care. Stopping or reducing heavy drinking suddenly can be dangerous for some people; if you drink heavily, talk to a doctor before making changes. If you are in crisis, contact emergency services.
