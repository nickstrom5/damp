How it works

One question a night. That's the whole habit.

1
Tell it your number, once.

How many drinks a week, what they cost you, and how many dry nights you want. Damp turns that into money and mornings, in your numbers, not ours.

2
Tap once a night.

At 9pm (or whenever you pick) your phone asks "Dry tonight?" Tap Dry right from the notification. Had a drink? Tap that and say how many. No guilt screen.

3
Watch it add up.

A streak of dry nights, this week against your goal, the money you kept. A card at 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 60, 100 and 365 nights. Post it or don't.

Questions

Is this a sobriety app?

No. Damp is for drinking less. If you're quitting completely, set your goal to 7 dry nights a week and it works for that too, but it will never call a drink a relapse.

What if I forget to log a night?

The next morning the home screen asks "Forgot last night?" with two buttons. Nights you never log don't count either way; they don't break your streak and they don't add to it.

How is "money kept" calculated?

You tell Damp your usual drinks per week and what a drink costs you. Every logged night is compared to that baseline. A dry night keeps a night's worth; a lighter night keeps the difference. It's your numbers, so it's as honest as you are.

Does it see what I drink or where?

No. It knows what you tap and nothing else. There's no account and no server.

Is this medical advice?

No. Damp is a habit tracker. If drinking feels out of control, please talk to a doctor. In the US, SAMHSA's free, confidential helpline is 1-800-662-4357.
