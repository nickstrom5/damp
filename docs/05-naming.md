# Naming: why "Damp"

Decided 17 September 2026. The app is **Damp**. The line is **Drink less. Not never.**

## What the name has to do

1. Say the promise in one word: less, not zero.
2. Survive a 3-second TikTok caption and a 7-second demo.
3. Be clear of the App Store results for "sober", "drink less" and "alcohol tracker".
4. Not sit on someone else's trademark.

## Why Damp

- "Damp lifestyle" / "damp January" is the TikTok term for exactly our user: someone who wants
  to drink less without quitting. The word already carries the positioning; we don't have to
  teach it.
- It's the honest opposite of every "Sober X" app. A drinking night in Damp is a number, not a
  relapse.
- App Store search for "Damp" returns humidity meters and dehumidifier controllers. Nothing in
  Health & Fitness or Lifestyle. No consumer brand.
- Four letters. Works as a handle, a domain and an app icon (the half-full drop).
- Button copy and share cards write themselves: **Tonight's dry** · *12 dry nights in a row* ·
  *$343 kept*.

## Rejected

| Name | Why not |
|---|---|
| Dryish | Sunnyside runs a "Dry(ish) January" challenge under that exact spelling. Confusion and a trademark argument with the market leader. |
| Dry | *Dry: A Drink Tracking App* and Alcohol Change UK's "Try Dry" both exist. Also says "zero", which is the wrong promise. |
| Sober-anything | Says quit. Buried under I Am Sober, Sober Time, Sober Sidekick. |
| Nightcap | Cute, but it's the name of a drink and of several bar-finder apps. |
| Mostly / Fewer | Tried 18 Sep as replacements when the first-choice domains were taken. Both free, neither as good: Damp is the audience's own word. The fix was a different domain, not a different name. |
| Tally / Less / Skip | Generic; each already has an app with that name in an adjacent category. |
| Clearhead | Describes the result, but two syllables longer and "Clear" is crowded. |

## Domain

Checked against the registries on 18 Sep 2026. `damp.app`, `getdamp.app`, `getdamp.com`,
`trydamp.app`, `trydamp.com` and `dampapp.com` are all registered.

| Candidate | .app | .com | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **usedamp** | free | free | **Chosen.** Neutral, the standard "use<name>" pattern, both TLDs open. |
| drinkdamp | free | free | On-topic and memorable, but "drink" in the domain invites ad-platform alcohol flags. Good second choice. |
| joindamp | free | free | Implies a community we deliberately don't have. |
| heydamp | free | free | Friendly, slightly off-brand. |
| dampapp | free | taken | Split ownership across TLDs. |

A registry "not found" is a strong signal, not a guarantee: a name can still be premium-priced
or reserved. Check the price at checkout.
