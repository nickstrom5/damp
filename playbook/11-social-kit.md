## 7. Reddit (value first, product second)

Post text, not links. Mention Damp only in a reply when asked. r/stopdrinking forbids
promotion; contribute there without ever naming the app.

**r/sobercurious, r/dryalcoholics:**
> Title: Counting nights instead of drinks is the only thing that stuck for me
> Body: the honest list. Drink-by-drink apps (stopped logging by week 2), Dry January cold (quit on the 9th), "only weekends" (weekends got bigger). What worked: one question at 9pm, dry or not, and a number I could see grow. Money got me further than health ever did. Happy to share what I use if anyone wants; the principle works with a notes app.

**r/personalfinance:**
> Title: I tracked what 4 dry nights a week actually saved me for 60 days
> Body: the numbers ($8 average, 10 a week baseline, $2,377/yr projected, what actually happened). Ask what others counted.

**r/soberoctober (October only):**
> Title: Day 7 check-in: how's everyone counting?
> Body: your card, your streak, one tip. Ask for theirs.
