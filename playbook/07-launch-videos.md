# The first 10 launch videos: shot lists

Every one of these is a format that already works in the sober-curious category. The job is to
record them, not to improve them. Record all ten in one evening (the 9pm notification is real,
so start at 8:45), then post 1–2 a day across two accounts.

Rules for all ten:
- Product visible within 2 seconds. No intro, no logo.
- Text hook on the first frame, 6 words or fewer.
- 7–15 seconds. Longer only if the format needs it.
- CTA is the app name on screen at the end, nothing else. Don't say "link in bio".
- Captions on. Most people watch on mute.
- Never show a drink being drunk. Ad platforms reject it and it's off-brand.

---

### 1. The demo (the one everything else points back to)
**Hook text:** "One tap. That's the whole habit."
**Shots:**
1. (0–1s) Lock screen. Notification slides in: "Dry tonight?" Hard cut.
2. (1–3s) Thumb long-presses, taps "Dry tonight ✓". Notification disappears.
3. (3–6s) Open the app. Streak ticks 11 → 12. "$137 kept" ticks up. Confetti.
4. (6–8s) End card: app name.
**Why it works:** Nothing to explain. This is the App Store preview too.

### 2. The reveal
**Hook text:** "I put my honest number in."
**Shots:**
1. Screen recording: slider to 10 drinks a week, $8 a drink.
2. Reveal screen: "$4,160" counts up. Cut to your face. Hold 1s.
3. "$2,377 back" counts up. Small nod.
4. End card.

### 3. Day 1 of Sober October
**Hook text:** "Day 1 of Sober October. Here's my tracker."
**Shots:**
1. Talking head, 1 sentence: "Not quitting forever. 31 nights. This is how I'm counting."
2. Tap "Tonight's dry". Confetti. "Dry night 1 · $11 kept" card.
3. "I'll post the card every 7 days."
4. End card.
**Note:** Post Oct 1. Then post the 7, 14, 30 cards on schedule. Four videos from one setup.

### 4. POV: 9pm
**Hook text:** "POV: 9pm, the group chat says 'one drink'"
**Shots:**
1. Phone on the couch. Messages: "one drink?" Notification slides over it: "Dry tonight?"
2. Thumb hovers. Taps "Dry tonight ✓".
3. Look at camera. "$11. And tomorrow morning."
4. End card: "Damp. Not never."

### 5. Slideshow: 5 signs
**Hook slide:** "5 signs you're drinking on autopilot"
**Slides:**
1. You don't decide to drink. It's just what 9pm is.
2. "One" is a word you say, not a number you hit.
3. Sunday is for feeling bad about Saturday.
4. You've said "not this week" more than once this month.
5. Slide 5: the home screen with 12 dry nights. "Count nights, not drinks."
**Music:** whatever's trending that week. Slideshows live on the sound.

### 6. Sunnyside vs. Damp
**Hook text:** "Sunnyside wants $99. Here's the $20 version."
**Shots:**
1. Sunnyside paywall screenshot. Hold 1s. "$99 a year for a text bot."
2. Cut: Damp paywall. "$19.99. No bot. One tap a night."
3. Home screen demo, 3s.
4. End card.
**Note:** Keep it factual. Sunnyside is a real product; we're cheaper and simpler, not better people.

### 7. The "I had a drink" screen
**Hook text:** "What 'I had a drink' looks like here"
**Shots:**
1. Home. Tap "I had a drink". Sheet: "How many tonight?" Tap 2. "Log 2 drinks".
2. Home: "Tonight: 2 drinks. Tomorrow's a fresh start." Streak 0. Savings still $120.
3. Caption: "No relapse. No shame screen. A number."
4. End card.
**Why:** The sober-curious audience's biggest fear is the guilt trip. Show there isn't one.

### 8. Saturday morning
**Hook text:** "Saturday 7am at 6 drinks vs. at 0"
**Shots:**
1. Grey, curtains shut, phone face-down. Text: "6 drinks".
2. Cut: light, coffee, phone shows "3 dry nights in a row". Text: "0 drinks".
3. End card.

### 9. The widget
**Hook text:** "My Home Screen has one button"
**Shots:**
1. Home Screen with the Damp widget: "12 nights · $120 kept · [Tonight's dry]".
2. Tap the button. App opens, confetti, 13.
3. End card.

### 10. Founder, day 1
**Hook text:** "I built the drink-less app that doesn't make you quit"
**Shots:**
1. Talking head. "Every app in this space is about sobriety. I just wanted fewer nights. So: one tap, a streak, and the money you didn't spend." (demo plays picture-in-picture)
2. "I'll post every day of Sober October. Day 1: 0 users."
3. End card.
**Why:** Build-in-public series give you a reason to post daily and a story people follow.

## What to measure after the first 20 posts

Per video: views, profile taps, App Store page views (App Store Connect → Analytics → Sources),
installs, trial starts. Put them in one spreadsheet. The video with the best **installs per 1,000
views** wins, regardless of total views. Make ten more of that one.
